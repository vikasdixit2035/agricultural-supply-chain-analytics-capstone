# What Is Unique in This Capstone

1. **Lot-level farm-to-customer traceability** — every allocated shipment can be traced back to the originating harvest lot and farm.
2. **Perishability economics** — shelf life, storage class, ageing, spoilage, expiry and customer rejection are treated as financial variables.
3. **Cold-chain analytics** — learners quantify temperature excursions, reefer compliance, transport loss and cold-chain investment opportunities.
4. **FEFO / shelf-life risk** — analysis goes beyond FIFO to First-Expired-First-Out logic, which matters for perishables.
5. **Agricultural yield risk** — expected vs actual yield, weather risk, irrigation and supplier reliability are connected to sourcing decisions.
6. **Procurement price variance** — farm purchase price is benchmarked against monthly market reference prices.
7. **Quality-at-source analytics** — grade, moisture/Brix, pesticide test status and rejected quantity become supplier-performance inputs.
8. **Demand forecasting** — learners build a product-level forecast and measure WAPE on a holdout period.
9. **Safety stock & reorder points** — demand variability and lead time are converted into inventory-policy recommendations.
10. **Working-capital vs spoilage trade-off** — more stock can improve availability but increases ageing and loss.
11. **Recall simulation** — learners must identify every customer/order exposed to a simulated contaminated lot.
12. **Consulting-style value sizing** — interventions are translated into low/base/high INR impact scenarios rather than vague recommendations.
