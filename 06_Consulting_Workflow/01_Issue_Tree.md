# Issue Tree — How can AgriFlow improve farm-to-customer margin and reliability?

1. **Source better** — yield reliability, quality acceptance, market-price variance, supplier concentration.
2. **Move better** — transit delay, freight productivity, reefer compliance, transport loss.
3. **Store better** — ageing, shelf-life risk, spoilage, cold capacity, FEFO discipline.
4. **Serve better** — fill rate, OTIF, customer rejection, lost sales.
5. **Plan better** — demand forecast accuracy, safety stock, reorder points, working capital.
6. **Respond faster** — lot traceability and recall containment.
