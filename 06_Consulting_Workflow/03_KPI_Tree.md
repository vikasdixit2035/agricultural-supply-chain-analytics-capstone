# KPI Tree

**Margin protection** = Procurement price + freight + quality loss + transport loss + spoilage + customer rejection.

**Supply reliability**: Yield attainment, quality acceptance, supplier score.  
**Logistics reliability**: Inbound delay, temperature excursion rate, transport loss, freight/kg.  
**Inventory health**: Age, days to expiry, spoilage rate/value, critical shelf-life stock.  
**Customer service**: Fill rate, OTIF, rejection rate, lost-sales proxy.  
**Planning**: Forecast WAPE, safety stock, reorder point, working-capital exposure.  
**Risk**: Recall affected customers/orders and traceability response time.
