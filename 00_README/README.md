# Agricultural Supply Chain Analytics — Consulting-Style Capstone

## Scenario
You are the analytics workstream supporting the COO, Chief Procurement Officer, Head of Quality, Head of Logistics and CFO of **AgriFlow Foods**, a synthetic multi-category agricultural sourcing and distribution business.

The company sources grains, fruits and vegetables from farms and cooperatives, moves products through ambient/cold-chain logistics, holds lot-level inventory, and serves retailers, processors, HORECA, wholesalers and export customers.

## End-to-end flow
Farm → Harvest Lot → Quality Inspection → Procurement → Inbound Transport → Warehouse / Cold Storage → Lot Allocation → Customer Order → Outbound Delivery → Customer Service.

## Tool flow
Raw CSVs → PostgreSQL / SQL → Python forecasting & analytical models → Excel scenario model → Power BI executive report → management recommendation.

The company and records are synthetic. The workflow reflects common consulting and supply-chain analytics practices and does not reproduce proprietary methods of EY, Bain, McKinsey, or any other firm.
