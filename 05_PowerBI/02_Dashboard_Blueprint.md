# Power BI — 7 Page Executive Report

1. **Executive Supply Chain Control Tower** — procurement, quality, logistics, spoilage, fill rate, OTIF, financial leakage.
2. **Farm & Supplier Performance** — yield, rejection, PPV, certification, supplier risk.
3. **Inbound Logistics & Cold Chain** — delay, carrier score, reefer compliance, excursions, freight/kg.
4. **Warehouse / Shelf-Life / FEFO** — ageing, days-to-expiry, spoilage, warehouse risk.
5. **Demand, Inventory & S&OP** — demand trend, forecast vs actual, WAPE, safety stock, reorder point.
6. **Customer Service & Margin** — fill, OTIF, rejection, gross-margin / landed-cost analysis.
7. **Traceability & Recall** — selected lot → farm → warehouse → orders → customers, plus recall exposure.
